# LuminaCore 24-Month Roadmap

| Period | Objective | Deliverable | Decision gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1–M3 | Architecture freeze | Reference model + benchmark | All metrics and interfaces defined |
| M4–M6 | Nonlinear-device study | Candidate comparison + thermal/dynamic model | At least one viable candidate or justified pivot |
| M7–M10 | Linear optical core | MZI/MRR model + tolerance analysis | Application error target met |
| M11–M14 | System co-design | Hardware-aware inference + full energy model | Credible system-level advantage |
| M15–M19 | Prototype | Fabricated/assembled test article + characterization | Measurement quality sufficient for E2E test |
| M20–M24 | Demonstration | E2E workload + baseline comparison | Reproducible result and public technical record |

## Budget discipline

The $250,000 Fellowship budget is a ceiling, not a predetermined burn rate. Spending is tied to milestone evidence and procurement quotes. Hardware-intensive expenditure accelerates only after simulation and risk gates support the decision.

## Strategic pivots

Potential pivots include changing the nonlinear element, reducing optical depth, changing the benchmark workload, adopting a hybrid photonic/electronic architecture, or targeting inference classes where optical parallelism is structurally valuable.

## Final deliverable

The end of the Fellowship should produce one of two credible outcomes:

1. a measured, reproducible photonic edge-AI advantage; or
2. a rigorous negative result identifying the physical/system bottleneck and the most promising next architecture.

Both outcomes are preferable to an unsupported performance claim.
