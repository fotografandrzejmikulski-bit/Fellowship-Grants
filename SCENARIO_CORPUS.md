# Scenario Corpus v0.1

The corpus begins with deterministic, machine-readable workloads and is designed to expand without changing the evaluator contract.

## Current scenarios

- `edge_proprioception_small.json` — compact dense transform representative of a constrained edge primitive.
- `edge_proprioception_stress.json` — higher-dimensional workload intended to expose scaling behavior.
- `calibration_stress.json` — workload reserved for calibration/error sensitivity experiments.

## Planned scenarios

- event-stream temporal filtering;
- IMU + optical-flow fusion;
- low-resolution visual feature projection;
- multi-layer nonlinear inference;
- temperature-drift and hysteresis stress;
- source-power and detector-noise sweeps;
- quantization and ADC/DAC sensitivity.

Scenario definitions are inputs, not evidence. Results become evidence only when generated by the evaluator under documented conditions.
