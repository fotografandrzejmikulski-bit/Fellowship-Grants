# Citation & Claim Policy

## Rule 1 — Primary evidence first

Prefer primary papers, official program pages, measured datasets, and original technical documentation.

## Rule 2 — No borrowed performance

A performance number from the literature must never be presented as a LuminaCore result.

## Rule 3 — Label the evidence

Use E0–E4 evidence levels as defined in `EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md`.

## Rule 4 — Include conditions

Every quantitative claim should identify relevant operating conditions, measurement boundary, comparator, and uncertainty where available.

## Rule 5 — Preserve negative evidence

Contradictory or unfavorable findings remain in the record when they affect architectural decisions.

## Rule 6 — No future tense masquerading as present tense

“Will build,” “target,” “hypothesis,” and “expected” are reserved for unvalidated work. “Measured,” “demonstrated,” and “validated” require evidence.

## Rule 7 — Review before grant submission

The grant narrative must be checked against the evidence ledger immediately before submission so that repository state and application claims cannot diverge silently.
